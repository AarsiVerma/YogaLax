Discover Yoga

What is Yoga?

Yoga is the science of right living and, as such, is intended to be incorporated in daily life. It works on all aspects of the person: the physical, vital, mental, emotional, psychic and spiritual. The word yoga means unity or oneness and is derived from the Sanskrit word yuj, which means to join.

On a practical level, yoga balances and harmonizes the body, mind and emotions through asana, pranayama, mudra, bandha, shatkarma and meditation.

Mudra

A mudra is a symbolic or ritual gesture or pose in Hinduism, Jainism and Buddhism.

1.
Jhana Mudra (Psychic gesture of Knowledge)

This is one of the most fundamental yoga mudras for increasing concentration and knowledge.

Tip of index finger touches thumb, palm facing downward. Other three fingers are extended

2.
Chin Mudra (Awareness)

This is one of the most effective mudras for physical and mental well-being

Tip of index finger touches thumb, palm facing upward. Other three fingers are extended.

3.
Anjali Mudra (Gesture of reverence)

Offering prayer

Palms touching, fingers pointing upward. May be placed in front of the sternum, the forehead, or overhead.

Pranayama

Pranayama extends and regulates prana — your vital life force — through breathing techniques that calm the mind and energize the body.

Types of pranayama we teach

1. Bhramari Pranayama

Also called Humming Bee Breath, this form of pranayama is particularly helpful for those who suffer from hypertension.

2. Kapalbhati Pranayama

This form of pranayama helps detoxify the body and clears energy channels.

3. Bhastrika Pranayama

Also called Bellows Breath, this form of pranayama helps pump in more oxygen into the body.

4. Nadi Shodan Pranayama

This alternate nostril breathing technique helps calm the mind and helps harmonise both hemispheres of the brain.

5. Shitali Pranayama

This form of pranayama helps reduce anxiety and helps cool the body.

6. Ujjayi Pranayama

This breathing technique requires you to breathe through your mouth while you sit in a cross-legged position and mimic the sound of the ocean.

7. Anulom Vilom Pranayama

This breathing technique helps relax the body and cleans the nasal passages.

8. Sheetkari Pranayama

This breathing technique helps cool the body
